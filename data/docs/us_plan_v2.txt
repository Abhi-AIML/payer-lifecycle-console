GOLD PPO PLAN: SUMMARY OF BENEFITS AND COVERAGE (SYNTHETIC)
Effective 01 Jan 2026. Version 2 (amendment effective 01 Nov 2026).

[Page 1] Section 1. Annual deductible
1.1 The annual deductible is $1,000 per member and applies to inpatient and outpatient services.
1.2 Preventive visits are not subject to the deductible.

[Page 2] Section 2. Coinsurance and out-of-pocket maximum
2.1 After the deductible, the plan pays 90% and the member pays 10% coinsurance for in-network services.
2.2 The out-of-pocket maximum is $6,000 per member per year.

[Page 3] Section 3. Copayments
3.1 Primary care or specialist office visit: $30 copay per visit. The deductible does not apply.
3.2 Physical therapy visit: $40 copay per visit. The deductible does not apply.

[Page 4] Section 4. Prior authorization
4.1 Total knee replacement requires prior authorization. Without it the service is denied.
4.2 Advanced imaging such as MRI requires prior authorization. Without it the service is denied.

[Page 5] Section 5. Exclusions
5.1 Cosmetic procedures are not covered.
5.2 Experimental treatments are not covered.

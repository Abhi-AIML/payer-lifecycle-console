SURAKSHA HEALTH GOLD POLICY: WORDING (SYNTHETIC)
Insurer: Suraksha General Insurance. TPA: MediLink TPA. Version 2 (amendment effective 01 Nov 2026).

[Page 1] Section 1. Sum insured
1.1 The sum insured is Rs. 5,00,000 per policy year.

[Page 2] Section 2. Room rent and proportionate deduction
2.1 Room rent is covered up to Rs. 7,000 per day.
2.2 If the room chosen costs more than the limit, all room-linked charges (surgeon, anaesthesia, nursing and operation theatre fees) are reduced in the same proportion. Implants, medicines, consumables and diagnostic tests are not room-linked and are not reduced.

[Page 3] Section 3. Procedure sub-limits
3.1 Total knee replacement is covered up to Rs. 2,50,000 per admission.
3.2 Cataract surgery is covered up to Rs. 50,000 per eye.

[Page 4] Section 4. Waiting periods
4.1 An initial waiting period of 30 days applies to all illnesses, except accidents.
4.2 Joint replacement is covered only after 2 years of continuous cover.

[Page 5] Section 5. Co-payment, non-payable items and exclusions
5.1 A co-payment of 5% applies to every admissible claim.
5.2 Non-medical items such as administrative charges, toiletries and consumables are not payable.
5.3 Cosmetic procedures are not covered.

[Page 6] Section 6. Cashless treatment
6.1 Cashless treatment requires pre-authorization from the TPA.
